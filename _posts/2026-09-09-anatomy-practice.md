After learning the basics of hand anatomy through research, I decided to go ahead and apply what I learned to some sketches.

I think it’s safe to say that I did not know what I was getting myself into.

One page worth of hand sketches was enough to make me question if I even knew what a human hand looked like. Sometimes, the fingers were too long, and other times, too short. I would also get the proportions wrong and make the palm too wide. For the most part, something just didn’t feel right, but I could never tell what exactly that thing was.

But over the span of a few days, I got the hang of it. While using my hands for reference allowed me to draw with greater anatomical accuracy, I still plan to practice basic hand gestures thoroughly before attempting more complex ones.
