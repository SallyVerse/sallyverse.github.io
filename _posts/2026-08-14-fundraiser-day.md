## Fundraiser
The fundraiser is *finally* over! We ran the market for an hour, and the other seventh grade class took over after us. Nearly all the items sold out thanks to our fifth grade and sixth grade customers. The booth I worked in was mainly just selling items such as plantable pencils, wax crunch rings, snacks, candy, and my very own artworks! Initially, I didn't get my hopes up thinking that no one would want to buy my original art, but I'm so glad they did. I priced the graphite drawings at 3,000 won, the small painting at 5,000 won, and the larger one at 9,000. Since multiple people wanted one of the graphite drawings and the larger painting, they both sold for higher than the original price!

30 minutes into the fundraiser, most of the items had already sold, so I moved to the busier booth to help out the two classmates working there. In this booth, we had a plant-simulation with brownies—yes, brownies—and a jellybean blind-tasting. I assisted with the brownies, professionally placing them on a plate and swirling the whipped cream on top with utmost grace 🙂. As expected, this station was the most successful one!

After our one-hour shift came to an end, we reimbursed everyone the money that they each put into the materials for the fundraiser. The total that we earned was a little over 330,000 won! We were so glad after seeing that number since we were expecting around half of that amount.

The number for donation was announced this week, and thanks to everyone's efforts, we were able to donate a grand total of 3.1 million won to Seoul Green Trust, nearly one million won more than last year's fundraiser.

Working with my entire class as well as another class was tough, and sometimes, I had doubts about how the fundraiser would go, but we found a way through it and all our efforts paid off!
