Through this assignment, I focused on practicing precision and value rendering. I chose a horizontal orientation for my subject since my subject is wider than it is long.
Starting off with a simple sketch, I ensured that my sushi was centered correctly and simplified it into five cylindrical forms. After the sketch was completed, I began to draw the sushi one by one, outlining the curves and drawing the shadows of the rice.

For the creative marks in the background, I decided to make an additional outline distanced just slightly from the subject to separate it from the background and create emphasis. However, I do not think this made the piece very cohesive as the outline is very simple while the subject is realistic.
