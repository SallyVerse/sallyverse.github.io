**Artist:** Sally Choi

**Title:** Algebra in Daily Life

**Medium:** Acrylic on canvas

**Date:** 07.31.2026 ~ 08.12.2026

The first thought that entered my brain when I started working on my Honors project was "How could I create an interdisciplinary piece that would incorporate both my knowledge of algebra and my passion for art?"

…That was a difficult question to answer.

At first I considered trying out Desmos art, which would have been very fascinating to try out. However, I didn't think I would be able to understand how to use Desmos, learn where to start, and create an art piece in the limited time that I had until the deadline. Thus, I went back to brainstorming.

A few hours later, I had it.

I would create a detailed painting with four sections that would each represent an ordinary moment in the world, and they would all tie back to algebra.
