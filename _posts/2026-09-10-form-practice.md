By practicing form and circles, I have become more confident in my line strokes and in my understanding of 3D objects. However, now that I look back on the first page, I can see that some of my contour lines all share the same degree, which is unrealistic. Due to perspective, the viewing angle for each line is different in reality.

On the bright side, the spheres on the second page give me confidence in my ability to draw a rather accurate circle on command 🙂.
