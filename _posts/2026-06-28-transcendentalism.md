**Artist:** Sally Choi

**Title:** Best Doods

**Medium:** Acrylic on canvas

**Date:** 06.8.2026 ~ 06.13.2026

This painting portrays me (left) next to my best friend (right). I gifted this painting to her as a birthday present since her birthday had passed when we last met up. I wanted this artwork to show just how deeply I care about our friendship and how we will always be "Best Doods". I'm sure the title "Best Doods" is a bit confusing, but I didn't want to write "best friends" on the canvas. That seemed a bit…boring. Instead, I went with "doods", and this wasn't a spontaneous decision to start spelling the word "dudes" incorrectly. When we message each other, we always call each other "doods". Although it started off as a fun typo, it ended up as our unofficial way of addressing each other.

Additionally, I used a reference image from two years ago, so we look a bit younger in the painting than we do now. However, I think it adds a nice touch. The visible difference in our younger image and our current selves depicts how much we've grown together over the years.
